Thermal seal inspection

Right after a bag is sealed, the seal is hotter than the rest of the bag. VeriSeal's thermal camera photographs that heat line on every bag. A continuous line is a good seal. A gap, a thin spot or product caught in the seam is a bad one. The result goes to the PLC as a good or bad signal, and the plant decides what happens next: a stack light, a line stop or a reject.

Bag tracking and packer attribution

On a line with several packers, the checkweigher and the metal detector each give a verdict on a bag, but neither knows which packer filled it. PackSentry puts a camera at each spout and along the conveyors. It counts each bag as it leaves its packer and follows it down the line, so every weight reading, every metal detect and every bag lost between stations is tied to the packer it came from.

Two packer test line. Each bag carries its packer from the spout to the roller table.

Every bag counted

Spout cameras see each bag drop. The target is every bag, not a sample.

Checkweigher feedback by packer

The checkweigher keeps its own targets and verdicts. PackSentry adds which packer the bag came from, so under and over counts show per packer.

Metal detector events by packer

A metal detect is traced back to the packer that filled the bag instead of being a line-level event.

Bags lost between stations

Counts at each station along the line show where bags go missing: at the turnover, on the incline or before the checkweigher.

Line board

One screen shows each packer and each conveyor stage with counts, bags per hour and losses. Built for the control room PC, not a phone.

Up to five cameras

PoE cameras on one edge computer at the line. Setup is the line name, the packer count and a name for each camera.
